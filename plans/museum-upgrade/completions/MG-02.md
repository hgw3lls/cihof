It preserves browser-local staff settings, prepared data, and the intentional persistent color theme. The reset works repeatedly and while already in People.

The public companion build has no inactivity reset by default. The kiosk build, or an explicitly requested `?kiosk=1` session, uses the configured kiosk timeout with a 30-second default warning. Pointer, keyboard, wheel, touch, and scroll input all share the same activity policy. QR no longer competes with a separate auto-close timer while open in the exhibit. Film playback extends the session only when time is progressing; paused, stalled, and ended media do not hold the session indefinitely. Pending transcript work is aborted when its film view unmounts.
