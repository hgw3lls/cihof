# Samuel H. Miller (2013): sources for the rewritten biography

Collected 2026-10-02 for the biography rewritten under
`biographies-review-2026-10-02`, at Tony Yanick's request, so that it reads
like the other rewritten biographies. Each fact in that biography that is not
in the Hall of Fame's own text for him is listed here with where it was read.
Nothing here is read by the pipeline; the biography itself is in
`data/cihof_curated_metadata.json`.

The Hall of Fame's own text supplies: co-chairman emeritus of Forest City
Enterprises; a key stakeholder since 1947; credited with the move into land
development; treasurer 1992, chairman 1993, co-chairman with Albert B. Ratner
1995; the boards and honorary trusteeships; The Cleveland Clinic board and
executive committee, and Distinguished Fellow 2003; Case Western Reserve
University and a Harvard MBA; U.S. Navy 1942 to 1946.
