# EV-08 — Places, stories, comparison and time, when they have content

**Depends on:** EV-05, EV-06
**Cases:** T02, T03, T04, T05, T09, T10, T12, T13, E-A6

## Goal

Each remaining interpretive surface ships when its content clears a stated
threshold, and not before.

## Why here, and why one stage

CE gives these six stages in a fixed order (CE-03 through CE-08). This plan
makes them one gated stage with four independent sub-deliverables, because their
order should follow review throughput, which nobody can predict today.

A lens below threshold is **absent from the build**, not present-and-empty. An
empty lens is not a deliverable, even when its emptiness is honest.

## Thresholds

Proposals. The content team sets the numbers (open question Q2); the tracker
records who set them and when.

| Surface | Proposed threshold | Current |
|---|---|---|
| Places | ≥ 8 of 14 seeds reviewed, role and evidence per association | 0 |
| Contribution stories | ≥ 20 people with an approved sourced beat | 0 |
| Comparison | ≥ 40 people with two or more comparable reviewed facts | 0 |
| Historical activity | ≥ 30 people with an approved activity range distinct from induction | 0 |

## Work

Take the CE stage prompts as the design source for each surface — they are good
descriptions of the destination and this plan does not rewrite them:

- Places → `cihof-city-experience/prompts/CE-05_cleveland-places.md`
- Stories and evidence → `CE-04_contribution-stories-and-evidence.md`
- Comparison → `CE-06_connections-and-comparison.md`
- Historical activity → `CE-07_historical-activity.md`

Apply this plan's constraints on top: D08 layering, the CE-02 rule that
discovery narrows an index and not a relationship graph, decision E03 gating,
and per-surface accessibility built in the same stage rather than deferred.

Schematic place markers are not geographic coordinates. Unknown does not become
present day. No score and no winner in comparison.

## Acceptance

For each surface that ships: its listed cases pass, and E-A6 holds — the
threshold, its value and its owner are recorded, and a below-threshold lens is
absent from the built artifact rather than empty in it.

## Completion

One completion record per surface shipped, `completions/EV-08-<surface>.md`;
update the tracker with the threshold actually met. Stop for review after each.
