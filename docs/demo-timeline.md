# Demo Timeline Alignment

This demo should be framed against the 2026 contract schedule:

- July 2026: kickoff, interface design, content review, and software architecture.
- August 2026: core software development and exhibition functionality.
- September 2026: content integration, testing, refinement, documentation, and client review.
- October 2026: final revisions, staff onboarding, and installation-ready delivery.

## Current Demo Position

The current app is best presented as a functional prototype moving into the September content-integration and review phase.

Demo-ready items:

- Explore, Timeline, Regions, Journeys, detail drawer, kiosk mode, and attract mode.
- 111 inductee records from 2010-2026.
- The official 2026 class imported from the Cleveland International Hall of Fame site.
- Primary images for every inductee.
- Video links for 64 inductees.
- Search and filters by name, year, region, media, and theme.

## Recommended Demo Flow

1. Open the Timeline view and show that the collection now runs through the 2026 class.
2. Select 2026 and open one profile from the newest class.
3. Use search to find a known inductee by name or community.
4. Switch to Regions to show geographic browsing.
5. Open Journeys to show the interpretive layer as a prototype feature.
6. Toggle kiosk mode and use Reset to show unattended-public-use behavior.

## Scope Boundaries

The October delivery version still needs final content approval, hardware-specific verification, staff documentation, and onboarding. Optional future-development ideas such as relationship intelligence, research mode, advanced CMS tools, and deeper historical context should be described as future phase work unless separately approved.

The app is designed for offline operation, but the current repository does not include downloaded local video files. Video links are present for the demo; a final kiosk package should include a media packaging/validation pass if offline video playback is required.
