# The Hall of Fame display: the card

Print this on one sheet, both sides, and keep it by the display. It covers
what staff do. Everything else is in the [gallery staff
walkthrough](gallery-staff.md), the [staff portal
walkthrough](staff-portal.md), the [display remote](display-remote.md) and
the staff guide (`STAFF-GUIDE.md`).

---

## Side 1: every day

**Opening.** Switch on the PC. The exhibit starts by itself. Leaving it on
is fine: it restarts itself every night at 04:00.

**The daily check.**

1. The attract screen is showing, one portrait lit up in colour.
2. Touch **Touch to enter**, then a face: their panel opens.
3. Touch **Search** and type a few letters on the screen's own keys: the
   matching faces light up.
4. Touch **Start over**: the attract screen comes back.
5. Wipe the screen with a soft dry cloth.

**The admin panel.** Hold a finger on the top-left corner of the screen for
5 seconds, then type the passcode and **OK**. (With a keyboard plugged in,
**Ctrl + Shift + A** does the same.) Five wrong tries lock the keypad for a
minute. **Close** it when you are done.

**Something looks wrong.**

1. Open the admin panel and write down **Release**, **Content**, the time
   and what you saw.
2. Touch **Reload**.
3. Still wrong: **Restart app**.
4. Still wrong: restart the PC, and report what you wrote down.

Frozen? Wait 30 seconds first: the exhibit mends a frozen page by itself.
The Windows desktop is showing: start **CIHOF Exhibit** from the Start menu.

**Never** copy the exhibit's files, or its installer, anywhere. They hold
films cleared for this display only.

## Side 2: changing something

For a misspelt name, a corrected biography, a new portrait. On the staff
computer with *CIHOF Staff Portal*, the same steps every time.

**1. Start from what the display shows.** In the admin panel: **Staff
connection**, **Open a staff connection** (or **Open on the display's own
Wi-Fi**, where the display has no network, and join the Wi-Fi it names). In
the portal: **Connect to a display**, type the address and code it shows
(or choose **On the display's own Wi-Fi** and type only the code).
*No network at all?* Admin panel: **Content › Export current content…** to a
USB stick; in the portal: **Open a display's content**.

**2. Make the change.** The first time, the portal asks your full name.
Touch **Edit**, then what you want to change on the exhibit. Change it,
**Save**.

**3. Approve it.** Touch **… waiting for approval**, look at each change,
**Approve** it.

**4. Publish.** **The display**, then **Make a display update**, with a line
saying what changed.

**5. Put it on the display.** Connected: **Send it to the display**, then
**Apply at the next reset**. On a stick: copy the file from **Documents ›
CIHOF display updates**; in the admin panel, **Content › Load a content
update…**, read what it changes, **Apply at the next reset**. (Or send it
from the *CIHOF Display Remote* phone app.)

**6. Look at the display.** Close the staff connection when you are done.

**It went wrong?** Admin panel, **Content**, **Versions kept**: **Show this
version** on the one before, or **Go back to it** for the content the
display was delivered with. Nothing is ever deleted.

**The display says the update was made from an earlier version?** Someone
changed the display after you started. Do not apply it. Start again at
step 1.
